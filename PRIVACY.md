# PERLA Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

PERLA is a virtual try-on and AI fashion creation service at https://studioperla.live. This notice describes the current service. Contact the PERLA operator at **raimjanovnursultan@gmail.com** with privacy questions or requests.

## Information we process

- Account details: email address, display name and profile information supplied during registration, including information returned by Google if you use Google sign-in.
- Photos and product images that you upload or select, current selections, generation requests, prompts, generated photos and videos.
- Actions such as likes, sharing, collections and answers to try-on feedback questions.
- Demo applications: your name, email, brand or company, optional message and consent to be contacted.
- Technical information needed to operate and troubleshoot the service, such as request status and error logs.

## Why we use it

We use this information to sign you in, restore your selections, generate and save your creations, support community features, respond to applications and support requests, and understand aggregate fashion preferences. Demo statistics are labelled as sample data and are not a verified count of real users.

Virtual try-on is a visual simulation, not a guarantee of physical size, fit or appearance. Only upload images you have permission to use.

## AI and service providers

Supabase supplies account, database and file-storage services. Perfect Corp processes images for virtual try-on and video generation. Gemini and the app's AI image service process relevant images or prompts for product classification and AI Studio. These services receive the inputs needed for the requested operation and handle them under their own terms and privacy notices. Processing may take place outside your country. PERLA cannot make guarantees about providers' retention or model-training practices beyond their applicable agreements.

The weekly insights workflow receives aggregated feedback and style signals, not your uploaded photos. Individual Style DNA reports are stored for the relevant account.

## Visibility and sharing

Uploaded source photos and personal product images are held in private storage with account-scoped access. Generated images and videos are currently stored in a public result bucket: **anyone with a direct result URL may access that file, even if you have not shared it in the community**. Do not generate sensitive images.

When you choose to share a creation, other signed-in members can see the image or video, displayed creator name, product information and like count. Unsharing removes it from community views but does not revoke a public file URL or copies others have already downloaded. Brand insights show aggregated fashion signals; community posts remain identifiable by their displayed creator name.

Demo applications are not publicly readable and can be reviewed by the PERLA operator in Supabase.

## Storage, retention and deletion

Account and uploaded-input records are retained to provide saved selections and history until removed by the operator or as part of an account-deletion request. Changing or removing a current photo/product selection does not necessarily delete its saved upload record.

Deleting a generated look in the app removes its saved job, linked video jobs, result files, likes and feedback. This cannot erase downloaded copies or cancel a request already submitted to an AI provider. For account deletion, source-upload removal, access or correction requests, email the address above. Provider logs and backups may follow separate retention schedules.

## Browser storage and security

The browser stores the Supabase session so you remain signed in. Access controls are used for private uploads and account records. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your account credentials private and sign out on shared devices.

## Your choices

You may choose not to upload personal photos and instead use the supplied sample models, avoid community sharing, delete generated looks, and contact PERLA about your information. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal data. Contact us to make a request or withdraw consent for demo-application contact.

## Updates

This notice may change as PERLA evolves. The date above identifies the latest revision. This policy does not create claims of legal compliance in every jurisdiction; the operator should obtain appropriate legal review before wider public launch.
